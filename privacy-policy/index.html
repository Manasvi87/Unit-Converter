Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains clearly what information this website collects, how conversions work in your browser, and which third-party services are used.

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how UnitConversion (“we”, “our”, or “the website”), accessible at https://manasvi87.github.io/Unit-Converter/, handles visitor information. By using this website, you agree to the practices described on this page.

2. Information Collected by the Website

We do not require user accounts, logins, registrations, or online contact form submissions to use the converter, and we do not operate a custom backend database to store personal visitor profiles.

Like most websites hosted on static hosting platforms, basic technical server logs (such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and timestamp) may be processed automatically by our hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) for security and delivery. In addition, if you choose to email us directly, we receive your email address and the contents of your message solely to respond to you.

3. Information Processed Locally in the Browser

All unit conversions—including numbers, category selections, unit choices, and copied results—are calculated locally on your device using client-side JavaScript. The values you type into the converter are not sent to or stored on any server operated by us.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

At this time, our website code does not set first-party tracking cookies and does not store personal information in your browser’s localStorage or sessionStorage. However, if third-party advertising or analytics services are enabled in the future, those partners may place or read cookies or similar browser identifiers as described below.

You can control or clear cookies at any time through your web browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

To style and deliver the website, pages load assets from the following external services:

  • Google Fonts: Used to load the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface from Google servers.
  • Font Awesome CDN (cdnjs / Cloudflare): Used to load interface icons.
  • GitHub Pages: Used to host and serve the static files of this website.

When your browser requests stylesheets, fonts, or pages from these third-party providers, standard network information such as your IP address and user-agent string is transmitted to those providers under their respective privacy policies.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

Current Status: Google AdSense advertisements are not currently active on this website.

If we integrate Google AdSense or other advertising partners in the future, those third-party vendors—including Google—may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Where applicable, Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

When advertising is enabled, visitors may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google’s Ads Settings or through regional industry opt-out tools, and we will update this section to reflect active ad integrations.

7. Analytics

We do not currently run third-party web analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics) on this site. If we add privacy-focused or standard analytics tools later to understand page traffic and improve usability, we will update this Privacy Policy before or at the time those tools are added.

8. Data Security

Because conversion calculations happen locally in your browser without user accounts or stored conversion histories, data exposure is minimized. Static pages are served over HTTPS where supported by GitHub Pages; however, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

This website is a general-purpose utility and educational tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information via email, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

10. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time—especially when adding new features, analytics, or advertising partners such as Google AdSense. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date at the top.

11. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page or email us directly at manasvistudent2006@gmail.com.
