Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the unit conversions?

Physical and mathematical conversions (length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, pressure, power, and number systems) use standard conversion constants and display up to 6 decimal places. For currency conversions, the tool uses built-in reference exchange rates for quick estimates—always verify live market rates before making financial decisions.

Why does temperature conversion work differently from length or weight?

Unlike length or weight, temperature scales do not share the same zero point. For example, 0 °C equals 32 °F and 273.15 K. Instead of a single multiplication factor, the converter applies exact offset formulas such as °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32 and K = °C + 273.15.

How do I use the Base / Num (Number System) converter?

Select the Base / Num tab, pick your input base (Decimal, Binary, Octal, or Hexadecimal), and enter a valid value for that base—for example, only 0 and 1 for Binary, or 0–9 and A–F for Hexadecimal. The tool automatically validates characters and converts to your target base.

Can I reverse units or copy my conversion result?

Yes. Click the circular amber swap button between the two unit boxes to flip the source and target units immediately. Once your result appears, click Copy Result to copy the full formatted conversion string to your clipboard.

What is the difference between metric and imperial/US units?

The metric system (meters, kilograms, liters) is decimal-based, scaling cleanly by powers of 10. Imperial and US customary units (inches, feet, miles, pounds, ounces, US gallons) use historical ratios such as 12 inches per foot or 16 ounces per pound. This tool bridges both systems automatically.

Is this converter free to use on mobile and desktop?

Yes, the unit converter is 100% free with no account or installation required. Its layout adapts automatically to smartphones, tablets, and desktop screens, and all calculations run locally in your browser.
