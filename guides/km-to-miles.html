How Do You Convert Kilometers to Miles?

Convert road distances, highway speed limits, and running race routes from metric kilometers (km) to statute miles (mi) with clear examples and mental-math tricks.

Understanding Kilometers and Miles

A kilometer (km) equals 1,000 meters and is the standard unit for road distances and geographic scale across most countries. A mile (mi)—specifically the international statute mile of 5,280 feet (or 1,609.344 meters)—is used for highway signs and speed limits in the United States, the United Kingdom, and several other territories.

Because 1 mile is longer than 1 kilometer (1 mi = 1.609344 km), a distance measured in miles always produces a smaller number than the same distance in kilometers.

Conversion Formula

miles = km × 0.621371

Multiply kilometers by 0.621371 (or divide by 1.609344) to get miles. The same multiplier also converts speeds from km/h to mph.

Worked Examples

Example 1: A 5K Fun Run (5 km)

5 × 0.621371 = 3.106855 mi—commonly cited by runners as 3.11 miles.

Example 2: A 10K Race (10 km)

10 × 0.621371 = 6.21371 mi, or about 6.21 miles.

Example 3: Full Marathon (42.195 km)

42.195 × 0.621371 = 26.21875 mi, which rounds to the familiar 26.22 miles.

Example 4: Highway Speed Limit (100 km/h)

100 × 0.621371 = 62.1371 mph, or roughly 62 mph.

  • International Driving & Car Rentals: Reading odometers and road signs accurately when driving across borders (for example, US-Canada or UK-continental Europe).
  • Running, Cycling & Fitness Apps: Translating Strava/Garmin splits and race distances between kilometers and miles.
  • Geography & Flight Planning: Estimating city-to-city overland distances on metric maps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Fibonacci trick for estimating km to miles?

Because the ratio between consecutive Fibonacci numbers (~1.618) is very close to 1.609344 km per mile, consecutive numbers in the sequence give quick estimates: 5 km ≈ 3 mi, 8 km ≈ 5 mi, 13 km ≈ 8 mi, 21 km ≈ 13 mi, and 55 km ≈ 34 mi.

How do I convert miles back to kilometers?

Multiply miles by 1.609344. For example, 10 mi × 1.609344 = 16.09344 km.

Is a nautical mile the same as a statute mile?

No. A statute (land) mile is 1.609344 km, whereas a nautical mile used in aviation and maritime navigation is 1.852 km (about 1.15 statute miles).
