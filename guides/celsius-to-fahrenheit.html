How Do You Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Understand the exact offset formula between °C and °F, convert weather forecasts, check body temperatures, and translate metric oven recipes in seconds.

Understanding Celsius and Fahrenheit

Celsius (°C) is the metric temperature scale used internationally for weather, science, medicine, and cooking. On the Celsius scale, pure water freezes at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C (at standard atmospheric pressure).

Fahrenheit (°F) is used primarily in the United States and a few Caribbean territories. On the Fahrenheit scale, water freezes at 32 °F and boils at 212 °F—a 180-degree interval compared to Celsius’s 100-degree interval. That means each 1 °C step equals 1.8 °F (or 9/5 °F), and the scales start at different zero points.

Conversion Formula

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Because 9 / 5 = 1.8, you multiply the Celsius temperature by 1.8 first, then add 32. Always multiply before adding 32!

Worked Examples

Example 1: Pleasant Spring Weather (20 °C)

(20 × 9/5) + 32 = 36 + 32 = 68 °F.

Example 2: Normal Human Body Temperature (37 °C)

(37 × 9/5) + 32 = 66.6 + 32 = 98.6 °F.

Example 3: Standard Baking Oven Setting (180 °C)

(180 × 9/5) + 32 = 324 + 32 = 356 °F (often rounded to 350 °F in US recipes).

Example 4: Sub-Zero Winter Cold (-15 °C)

(-15 × 9/5) + 32 = -27 + 32 = 5 °F.

Real-World Applications

  • Cooking & Baking: Converting European or UK oven dials (°C) into US oven settings (°F) and checking meat thermometer target temperatures.
  • Weather & Travel: Packing the right clothes when international weather apps show 15 °C or 30 °C.
  • Healthcare & Thermometers: Interpreting digital thermometer readings when traveling abroad (for example, recognizing 38.5 °C as 101.3 °F fever).
  • Aquariums, Pools & PC Hardware: Checking water heaters or CPU/GPU thermal specs across documentation written in °C or °F.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what temperature are Celsius and Fahrenheit equal?

They are equal at -40°: (-40 × 9/5) + 32 = -72 + 32 = -40 °F.

How can I estimate Celsius to Fahrenheit quickly in my head?

Double the Celsius number and add 30. For 20 °C: (20 × 2) + 30 = 70 °F (very close to the exact 68 °F). This shortcut works well for everyday weather between 0 °C and 30 °C.

How do I convert Fahrenheit back to Celsius?

Subtract 32 first, then multiply by 5/9: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. For example, (77 − 32) × 5/9 = 25 °C.
