How Do You Convert Meters to Feet?

A practical guide to translating metric meters into imperial and US customary feet, complete with worked examples, a quick reference table, and tips for reading decimal feet versus feet and inches.

Understanding Meters and Feet

The meter (m) is the base unit of length in the International System of Units (SI) used across almost the entire world for architecture, engineering, athletics, and everyday distances. The foot (ft) belongs to the imperial and US customary measurement systems and remains standard in the United States, aviation altitude reporting, and many real-estate listings.

Since 1959, one international foot has been defined as exactly 0.3048 meters (12 inches at 2.54 cm per inch). Dividing 1 by 0.3048 gives approximately 3.28084 feet per meter.

Conversion Formula

feet = meters × 3.28084

Multiply any measurement in meters by 3.28084 to get the length in decimal feet. If you need feet and inches (for human height), keep the whole number as feet and multiply the decimal remainder by 12 to obtain inches.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Standard Room Ceiling Height (2.7 meters)

2.7 × 3.28084 = 8.858268 ft. That equals roughly 8.86 feet (or 8 feet 10.3 inches).

Example 2: Personal Height (1.75 meters)

1.75 × 3.28084 = 5.74147 ft. The whole number is 5 feet, and multiplying the remainder 0.74147 × 12 gives 8.9 inches (about 5 ft 9 in).

Example 3: Olympic Swimming Pool Length (50 meters)

50 × 3.28084 = 164.042 ft, or just over 164 feet.

Example 4: Small Backyard Width (8 meters)

8 × 3.28084 = 26.24672 ft, which is approximately 26.25 feet.

Real-World Applications

  • Architecture & Interior Design: Comparing European or metric furniture dimensions and ceiling heights against US building blueprints.
  • Sports & Athletics: Understanding track-and-field events (100 m dash, long jump) or pool lengths (25 m vs. 25 yd) in feet.
  • Aviation & Drones: Translating international metric elevation maps into feet above mean sea level (MSL).
  • Height & Medical Forms: Converting metric passport or medical height records (1.75 m) into feet and inches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is 5.74 feet not 5 feet 7.4 inches?

Decimal feet divide one foot into 100ths, whereas traditional feet and inches divide one foot into 12 inches. To convert 0.74 feet into inches, multiply 0.74 × 12 = 8.88 inches (so 5.74 ft is roughly 5 ft 9 in, not 5 ft 7 in).

How can I estimate meters to feet in my head?

Multiply meters by 3.3 (or triple the number and add 10%). For example, 10 m × 3 = 30, plus 10% (3) = 33 ft—very close to the exact 32.81 ft.

How do I convert feet back to meters?

Multiply feet by 0.3048 (or divide by 3.28084). For example, 10 ft × 0.3048 = 3.048 meters.
