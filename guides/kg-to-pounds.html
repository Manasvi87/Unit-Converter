How Do You Convert Kilograms to Pounds?

Everything you need to convert metric kilograms (kg) into imperial/US pounds (lb) accurately for airline luggage limits, gym weights, shipping labels, and everyday measurements.

Understanding Kilograms and Pounds

The kilogram (kg) is the SI base unit of mass used worldwide in science, commerce, and everyday life. One kilogram equals 1,000 grams. The pound (lb)—specifically the international avoirdupois pound—is used across the United States and in certain contexts in the UK and Canada for body weight, groceries, and freight.

By international agreement, one pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, making 1 kilogram equal to roughly 2.20462 pounds.

Conversion Formula

pounds = kg × 2.20462

Multiply the mass in kilograms by 2.20462 to obtain the weight in decimal pounds. If a shipping label or kitchen scale requires pounds and ounces, multiply the decimal remainder by 16 (since 1 pound = 16 ounces).

Worked Examples

Example 1: Standard Checked Luggage Allowance (23 kg)

23 × 2.20462 = 50.70626 lb. That is just above the common 50 lb domestic US baggage limit (~50.71 lb).

Example 2: Olympic Barbell (20 kg)

20 × 2.20462 = 44.0924 lb—commonly rounded to 44.1 lb (close to a standard 45 lb US barbell).

Example 3: Carry-On Bag Limit (7 kg)

7 × 2.20462 = 15.43234 lb, or approximately 15.43 lb.

Example 4: Bulk Flour Bag (5 kg)

5 × 2.20462 = 11.0231 lb, or about 11 lb 0.4 oz.

Real-World Applications

  • International Air Travel: Avoiding overweight baggage fees when airlines list limits in kg (e.g., 20 kg or 23 kg) while home luggage scales read in pounds.
  • Strength Training & CrossFit: Converting competition kettlebells and calibrated metric plates (10 kg, 15 kg, 20 kg, 25 kg) into pounds to track personal records.
  • E-Commerce & Shipping: Preparing international customs forms and courier quotes across metric and US postal tiers.
  • Cooking & Nutrition: Scaling bulk ingredient bags or macro targets listed in kilograms vs. pounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a quick mental shortcut to convert kg to pounds?

Double the kilogram value, then add 10% of that doubled number. For example, for 60 kg: 60 × 2 = 120, plus 10% (12) = 132 lb (exact value: 132.28 lb).

Why is "lb" used as the abbreviation for pound?

"lb" comes from the Latin word libra, referring to the Roman unit libra pondo ("a pound by weight"), which gave English both the word "pound" and the symbol "lb".

How do I convert pounds back into kilograms?

Divide pounds by 2.20462 or multiply by 0.453592. For instance, 150 lb × 0.453592 = 68.04 kg.
