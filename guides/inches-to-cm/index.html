How Many Centimeters Are in an Inch?

Convert inches to centimeters with the exact 2.54 factor—ideal for screen diagonals, clothing size charts, woodworking hardware, and print dimensions.

Understanding Inches and Centimeters

The inch (in) is a small unit of length in the imperial and US customary systems (1/12 of a foot). Even in metric-majority countries, inches remain common for display diagonals (smartphones, laptops, TVs), bicycle wheels, plumbing pipes, and jeans waist sizing.

The centimeter (cm) equals one hundredth of a meter (10 millimeters). Since the International Yard and Pound Agreement of 1959, one inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters—there is no rounding error in this conversion factor.

Conversion Formula

cm = inches × 2.54

Multiply any length in inches by 2.54 to obtain the exact length in centimeters. If your ruler uses fractions such as 1/4 in (0.25) or 3/8 in (0.375), convert the fraction to a decimal first, then multiply by 2.54.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Smartphone Screen Diagonal (6.5 inches)

6.5 × 2.54 = 16.51 cm.

Example 2: Standard Laptop Display (15.6 inches)

15.6 × 2.54 = 39.624 cm (almost 40 cm diagonally).

Example 3: Waist Measurement (32 inches)

32 × 2.54 = 81.28 cm.

Example 4: Living Room TV (55 inches)

55 × 2.54 = 139.7 cm diagonal.

Real-World Applications

  • Online Clothing & Tailoring: Matching US waist, inseam, and chest measurements (inches) to international garment size charts (cm).
  • Electronics & Monitors: Checking whether a 14″, 15.6″, or 27″ screen fits inside a laptop sleeve or desk space measured in centimeters.
  • DIY, Furniture & Framing: Converting 4×6″ or 8×10″ photo frames, shelf brackets, and hardware dimensions for metric rulers.
  • Shipping & Carry-On Bags: Verifying small parcel and personal-item dimensions against airline limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1 inch exactly 2.54 cm, or is that rounded?

It is exact. By international definition adopted in 1959, 1 inch is defined as precisely 2.54 centimeters (25.4 millimeters).

How do I convert fractional inches like 3/8 inch to cm?

Divide the fraction first (3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 in), then multiply by 2.54: 0.375 × 2.54 = 0.9525 cm (9.525 mm).

How do I convert centimeters back to inches?

Divide centimeters by 2.54 (or multiply by 0.393701). For instance, 30 cm ÷ 2.54 ≈ 11.81 inches.
