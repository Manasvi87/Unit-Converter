How Many US Gallons Are in a Liter?

Convert metric liters to US liquid gallons accurately, learn why US gallons and UK Imperial gallons are not the same size, and review fuel, aquarium, and hydration examples.

Liters, US Gallons, and Imperial Gallons Explained

The liter (L) is a metric unit of volume equal to 1,000 milliliters or 1 cubic decimeter (a cube measuring 10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm). It is used globally for beverages, automotive fuel, and laboratory liquids.

US Liquid Gallon vs. UK Imperial Gallon: There are two different “gallons” in common use! A US liquid gallon equals 3.78541 liters (231 cubic inches, or 128 US fluid ounces). By contrast, a UK Imperial gallon equals 4.54609 liters (160 Imperial fluid ounces)—making an Imperial gallon roughly 20% larger than a US gallon. Our converter and the formula below use US liquid gallons.

Conversion Formula

US gallons = liters × 0.264172

Multiply the volume in liters by 0.264172 to find US liquid gallons (or divide liters by 3.78541). If you specifically need UK Imperial gallons, multiply liters by 0.219969 instead.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Two-Liter Soda Bottle (2 L)

2 × 0.264172 = 0.528344 US gallons—just over half a US gallon (about 2.11 US quarts).

Example 2: Standard Jerrycan (20 L)

20 × 0.264172 = 5.28344 US gallons (whereas in Imperial gallons, 20 L is ~4.40 Imp gal).

Example 3: Compact Car Fuel Tank (50 L)

50 × 0.264172 = 13.2086 US gallons, or roughly 13.21 US gal.

Example 4: Home Aquarium (100 L)

100 × 0.264172 = 26.4172 US gallons—close to a standard 25–29 gallon fish tank.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a US gallon smaller than a UK Imperial gallon?

The US gallon was standardized in 1776 based on the older English wine gallon (231 cubic inches, or 3.78541 L). In 1824, Britain replaced earlier gallons with the Imperial gallon defined as the volume of 10 pounds of water (4.54609 L), creating a ~20% difference.

How many liters are in 1 US gallon?

One US liquid gallon equals 3.78541 liters (about 3.79 L). Five US gallons equal roughly 18.93 liters.
